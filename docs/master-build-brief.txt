**CARA must not be publicly accessible.**

## 9. PRIVATE INVITATION SYSTEM

Only Give Holidays administrators may authorize access to CARA.

The process must work as follows:

1. Hotel submits a public partnership inquiry.
2. Administrator reviews the hotel.
3. Administrator approves the hotel contact.
4. The system sends a secure invitation by email.
5. The approved contact verifies access through the invitation process.
6. The hotel can access only its own private CARA submission.
7. Administrator reviews the completed submission.

Do not require hotel partners to create permanent accounts at launch.

Use secure, expiring, revocable, single-purpose invitation credentials with server-side validation.

Do not implement security through hidden links alone.

Do not expose access tokens in logs or analytics.

Include appropriate email verification before permitting confidential access.
